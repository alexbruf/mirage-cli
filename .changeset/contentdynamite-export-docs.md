Add `ve-dynamite articles export-docs <id> [--replace]`: export a finished article to a Google Doc in the operator's connected Google Drive, where Content Dynamite tracks the writer's edits and QA comments. When no Google account is connected, the error prints the connect link. Also carries `evals run` showing the format checks and rubric, and the QA judges running together. Lockstep 1.1.0.
